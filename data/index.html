Data

The files behind every number on this site. Each is what the run wrote, not a summary prepared afterwards.

Superseded files are here too and marked. A result that was withdrawn is part of the record, and removing it would undo the point of keeping a corrections log. Where a number moved, both versions are present and the log says what moved it.

  • abl1-holdout-set-v2.json 8 KB

    The ABL1 held-out set: 30 compounds in 30 chemical series, one per ECFP4 Tanimoto cluster at 0.6, with each measured affinity and the number of assays behind it.

  • abl1-holdout-predictions-v2.json 14 KB

    What the pipeline predicted for those 30, with pose confidence and the disagreement between the two affinity modules.

  • abl1-holdout-stats-v2.json 267 B

    The scores: Spearman, interval, mean absolute error, and the effective number of independent series.

  • abl1-holdout-set.json 8 KB superseded by abl1-holdout-set-v2.json

    The first ABL1 set, built by Bemis-Murcko scaffold. It admitted eight members of one congeneric series.

  • abl1-holdout-stats.json 121 B superseded by abl1-holdout-stats-v2.json

    Scores from that first set, Spearman 0.563. Here because it was published at the time and later withdrawn.

  • ache-holdout-set.json 8 KB

    The acetylcholinesterase set, same rules, an unrelated fold.

  • ache-holdout-predictions.json 14 KB

    Predictions for it. Every compound came back below the lowest pose confidence seen anywhere in the ABL1 set.

  • ache-holdout-stats.json 260 B

    Scores for acetylcholinesterase.

  • paired-validation.json 1 KB

    Direction of an affinity change across 4,899 within-target pairs, split by how large the real change is, against free energy perturbation on the subset where both have a value.

  • conformal-validation.json 3 KB

    Whether the prediction intervals cover what they claim to, per target, calibrated within a target and across targets.

  • fep-benchmark.json 6 KB

    Fifteen FEP+ targets, 364 compounds, scored within target and aggregated, with the arm comparable to the published figure marked.

  • abl1-crossover-v2.json 2 KB

    How a ligand-only model's held-out score changes with training set size, against the structure arm.

  • ntab-seed-variance.json 2 KB

    Thirteen seeds of one model on one benchmark tier, against the published spread between the three methods that benchmark ranks.

  • admet.json 12 KB

    Twenty-two TDC ADMET endpoints, one configuration, with the split digest for each.

  • abl1-control-card.md 8 KB

    The control battery on ABL1 as a card: every arm, every interval, and the verdict.

  • error-sources.json 5 KB

    Three registered questions about our own error: whether either of the model's uncertainty signals predicts it, how much of it is measurement disagreement, and which target properties explain performance. Two nulls and one confound in our own headline number.

  • 2026-10-09-error-sources.md 6 KB

    The pre-registration for that analysis, written before any of it was computed, including the decision rule and the correction for having tested five explanations.

  • temporal-holdout.json 3 KB

    Whether accuracy survives on recently published compounds. The primary test could not be run and is reported as such.

  • 2026-10-09-temporal-holdout.md 4 KB

    Its pre-registration.

  • corpus-28-targets.json 12 KB

    Every target the unattended worker has scored and every one it declined with the reason attached: twenty eight scored, eleven declined, the aggregate interval over targets, and the class A GPCR comparison.

  • md-week1-validation.json 5 KB

    The energy conservation check on the molecular dynamics module: one solvated protein run twice under NVE at two timesteps, on both GPU platforms, with the second order scaling test that establishes the integration is sound.

  • ca2-holdout-set.json 10 KB

    The carbonic anhydrase 2 held-out set: 40 compounds in 40 ECFP4 clusters.

  • ca2-holdout-predictions.json 19 KB

    Predictions for it, with interface confidence per compound. This is the run that retracted the pose trust signal.

  • CORRECTIONS.md 14 KB

    Every number that moved after it was stated, and what moved it.
