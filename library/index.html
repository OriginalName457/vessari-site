Simulation library

Every protein we hold a predicted pose for, simulated as a complex and played back frame by frame. 0 proteins so far, 0 of them sitting in a lipid bilayer.

The compound shown for each protein is the one this pipeline predicted most accurately, and that compound's error is on the card. The full record for every protein, including the ones we predict badly, is on the protein page.

A protein that crosses a membrane is built into a POPC bilayer. The rest are built in water. The builder decides from UniProt and then checks the structure itself, because an annotation describes a gene and we simulate a structure. Tumour necrosis factor is annotated transmembrane, correctly, and the form that binds drugs is the shed soluble trimer, so it is here in water.

These runs are short and made to be watched. None of them is a measurement.

    Each viewer loads only when you ask for it. Water, ions and lipid are stripped from the playback, and the residues within 5 angstroms of the compound are drawn in full.
