Engineering notes

Longer write-ups of single experiments, in more detail than the assessment page carries.

These are working notes rather than papers. They are written as the experiment happened, including the parts that did not work, and they are not revised afterwards to look tidier than the work was.

  • What a prediction costs 642 words

    Sixty two seconds and 4.17 watt hours of card energy per compound, measured per stage on the rig rather than divided out of a total. Three cards do not make it three times faster, because one folding job reserves 11.2 GB of a 15.9 GB host. A thousand compounds is under a day and ten thousand is eight days.

  • Our error in kilocalories 719 words

    Our 4.6 fold median error is 0.904 kilocalories per mole, and that number cannot be laid against a free energy perturbation result. This gives the conversion, the three reasons the comparison fails, and the one number measured the same way on the same pairs: our pairwise RMSE of 1.31 kcal/mol against 1.012 for FEP+ on the 518 pairs both scored.

  • The receptors were not harder 1,285 words

    The corpus went from twelve scored proteins to twenty eight. Sixteen of the new ones are class A G protein coupled receptors, folded from sequence with no membrane and no experimental structure, which we expected to be where this stops working. They scored no worse than the soluble enzymes. Mean Spearman 0.550, interval [0.467, 0.625], and the one negative entry in the corpus is a paralogue of a protein that scored +0.513.

  • Twelve targets instead of two superseded by the twenty eight target note 1,412 words

    An unattended run scored the pipeline against twelve proteins, taking the corpus from two. Mean Spearman 0.538 with a bootstrap interval over targets, one target that failed outright, eleven corpus entries deleted, and a length rule withdrawn.
